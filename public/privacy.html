3. Payment Data

4. Push Notifications

If you opt in to push notifications, your device's push subscription endpoint is stored on our secure servers solely to send you booking confirmations, invitation alerts, payment reminders, and trip updates. You can disable push notifications at any time through your device or browser settings.

5. Limited Sharing

Your information may be shared only where reasonably required to fulfil an experience — for example with transport partners, accommodation providers, or activity hosts — and only to the extent necessary. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

7. Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address to confirm your booking identity. We do not access any other Google account data beyond these basic profile details.

8. Data Storage and Security

Your information is stored on secure servers hosted by Supabase and Vercel (industry-standard cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest). Access to your personal data within our organisation is restricted to authorised personnel only.

9. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account remains active and as required to provide our services. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Booking and payment records may be retained for up to 7 years as required by Indian tax and accounting regulations.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

11. DND / Opt-Out

If you wish to stop receiving SMS, email alerts, push notifications or WhatsApp messages from us, send an email to chapteraaa.official@gmail.com with your mobile number, and we will remove you from our communication lists within 48 hours.
