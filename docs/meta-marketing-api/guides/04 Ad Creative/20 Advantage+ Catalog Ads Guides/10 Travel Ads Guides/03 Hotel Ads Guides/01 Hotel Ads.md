# Hotel ads


Facebook's hotel ads use cross-device intent signals to automatically promote relevant hotels from your inventory with unique creatives on Facebook.

## Set up hotel ads
1. [Create a hotel catalog containing your hotel inventory](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/hotel-ads/catalog)
2. [Set up hotel events on your website and in your app](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/hotel-ads/events)
3. [Build audiences to target with your Advantage+ catalog ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/travel-ads/audience-management)
4. [Create and start your hotel ads](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/marketing-api/dynamic-ads-for-travel/ads-management)
5. [Use hotel template tags in your ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/hotel-ads/template-tags)

## Advanced
1. [Set up dynamic (date-specific) pricing for your hotels](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/hotel-ads/dynamic-pricing)
