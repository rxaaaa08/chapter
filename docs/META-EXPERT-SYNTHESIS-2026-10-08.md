## 4. Strong consensus

"Voices" are independent people or shows.

1. **Simplify. Every added control must solve a named problem.** One campaign,
   few ad sets, leave age, gender and placements alone when optimising for a
   sale. *Loomer (dozens of posts), Heath, Hott, Graham, Winter, Foxwell blog,
   and a digest of 32 sources whose clearest signal for July–August 2026 was
   "consolidation beats complexity".* Matches YouTube pattern #8.
2. **Wait for enough data; panic costs more than a bad ad.** *Loomer, Heath,
   Graham, Winter.* Matches #11.
3. **Do not pause ads on their own cost per sale.** Spend is the system's vote.
   One ad taking most of the budget is normal. *Hott, Heath, Loomer, Tier 11
   (four episodes), Coffy.* Matches #5, #6, #7.
4. **Creative variety — but 2026 corrected 2025.** Late 2025 said "more ads".
   By mid-2026: variety is not volume. *Loomer: start with two or three ads.
   Scalability School: "stop making more ads", ask what the volume points at.
   Marketing Operators: volume only with clearer ideas.* Matches #2.
5. **Manual controls are leaving; "value rules" are the approved substitute.**
   *Loomer (ten-plus posts), Bram, Rahmey.* Adds to #8.
6. **Targeting inputs are only suggestions now, and separate retargeting is
   mostly unnecessary — but label your customers so you can see who you
   reach.** *Loomer, Hott, Heath, Coffy, Bram.* **Five voices; not a named
   pattern in the YouTube synthesis.**
7. **What Ads Manager credits is not what the ads caused.** *Kory, Hott,
   Rahmey, Graham, Tier 11, Holiday, Foxwell blog ("stop chasing the true
   number; make numbers useful").* Matches #14.
8. **The page after the click is part of the ad system.** Meta reads it; page
   variety counts as creative variety; the offer matters more than the ad.
   *Scalability School, Marketing Operators, the 32-source digest, Winter,
   Graham, Holiday.* Extends #13.
9. **Meta changes your ad unless you stop it.** Named this year: related media,
   AI translation, rewriting the text on images, AI summaries and comments, its
   own landing page, auto-added events, auto-added Pixel detail. *Loomer, Bram,
   Herrmann, Tier 11.* Matches #15.
10. **For a small or local account: optimise for the real sale from day one.**
    No account "warm-up". Loomer, 7 Oct 2026: when sales are too few, testing
    more, restricting more, or optimising for an earlier step "almost always"
    makes it worse. *Loomer, Heath, Graham, Winter.*

## 5. Real disagreements, and what decides them

| | One side | Other side | What decides it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **Launch many ads.** Tier 11, Mandalia; Motion's data (more ads, more winners); Coffy (an average ad gets 8 days of spend) | **Start with few.** Loomer, Scalability School, Marketing Operators | Budget. With few sales the system ignores most ads anyway. Note who sells creative |
| B | **Use incremental attribution.** Haus data (Kory), Faris, Loomer ("most advertisers should only use it") | **Tried it at $200K a month and switched it off.** Scalability School | Unresolved. Nobody reports it at small volume |
| C | **"Meta is having bad days."** Coffy (a tracker; "tanked in September 2026"), Herrmann, Hott | **"Stop saying Meta is broken."** Faris, Loomer (five episodes) | Whether you hold an independent count. Even Loomer's own account was wrongly restricted in June |
| D | **Manual bids and cost caps work.** Faris | **Sceptical.** Loomer; Holiday brought $1.4B of data to the debate | Not read in enough depth to call. YouTube pattern #19 still stands |
| E | **Exclude existing customers from prospecting.** Hott | **Don't restrict; just label and watch.** Loomer | Spend. Hott puts his own threshold at $50k a month |
| F | **Let AI act in the account.** Herrmann has it create lift studies | **Careful.** Coffy (a client lost $72,000 following AI advice); Loomer; Holiday ("no point of view" without business context) | Whether a human-set limit sits outside the AI |

## 6. What Meta changed, October 2025 → October 2026

| Month | Change |
|---|---|
| Oct 2025 | A small share of budget may reach placements you excluded |
| Nov 2025 | "First conversion" optimisation; Meta's large ad-ranking model described publicly |
| Jan 2026 | Reels reported to overtake the rest of Instagram's ad space |
| Feb 2026 | Detailed targeting and lookalikes become suggestions, then lose even their priority; first AI agent inside Ads Manager |
| Mar 2026 | **A click now only counts for attribution if it was a click on the link** (3 Mar). Reports of ad accounts shut down after being connected to AI tools. Overspend during an outage (16 Mar) |
| Apr 2026 | Free one-click Conversions API; "upgraded" Pixel that adds page detail by AI; bid adjustments by audience; **official AI Connectors for Claude and ChatGPT (29–30 Apr)** |
| May 2026 | Up to 10 images or videos in one ad; purchase audiences kept 730 days |
| Jun 2026 | Meta's AI agent in Instagram DMs and WhatsApp; "customer lifecycle" block on the ad set |
| Jul 2026 | Audiences reorganised around labels; Meta can rewrite the text on ad images; a wave of account bans |
| Aug 2026 | Custom attribution; permission controls for AI connectors (11 Aug); **placement exclusions start leaving ad sets (20 Aug)**; automatic DM reply to ad comments |
| Sep 2026 | New automatic events; Meta's own landing page option; paid business plans with limits on organic links; a broad performance dip reported from August |
| Oct 2026 | Meta's assistant can create campaigns and change budgets and targeting by conversation (6–7 Oct) |

Loomer counted 83 changes in 2025 alone.

## 8. Where it touches chapter அ — to check, not decisions

Nothing below was checked against the account. Ordered by how much it could
matter before any money is spent.

**Could change something**

1. **The AI connector and the ad account.** In March 2026 several advertisers
   reported accounts shut down after connecting them to Claude or similar tools;
   one lost 17. Meta made connectors official on 29–30 April and added
   permission controls on 11 August. The connector is plugged into Claude in
   this project and was used twice this session, read-only, for Help Center
   lookups. Worth confirming in Business Suite that it is held to view-only.
2. **Placement step in the Founders Meet plan** — already flagged in that plan.
3. **Attribution.** The link-click rule (March 2026) changes what "7-day click"
   counts. The ad set also now offers three models: standard, incremental,
   custom. `META-ADS-HANDOFF.md` mentions neither incremental nor custom
   attribution anywhere.
4. **Things Meta may now add to our tracking by itself:** the upgraded Pixel's
   page detail, automatic events, one-click Conversions API. We run our own
   server-side sending with a send guard; a second sender beside it is the
   risk.
5. **What Meta sees on our landing page.** Several sources say the system reads
   the page. Ours is a single-page app, and the handoff records that the page
   address sent to Meta is only ever the bare site address.
6. **Customer and engaged-audience labels** — five voices; Loomer's definitions
   are in §7.
7. **Default changes to ads:** related media, AI translation, image-text
   rewriting, Meta's own landing page. Check each against the enhancements guard.

**Supports decisions already made**

8. Purchase first, not an earlier step (Loomer, 7 Oct 2026). His episode names
   two fallbacks if that is still not enough; **the summary does not say what
   they are** — the one item here worth hearing in full.
9. Three ads to start; one ad set; judge the campaign, not single ads; no
   warm-up for a new account.
10. A spend alarm built on real spend and real tickets, given disagreement C.

**For later**

11. Automatic DM reply to ad comments — the worry about undecided visitors.
12. Limits on organic links, and clickable links on Reels (March 2026) — our
    funnel is organic reels.
13. Custom attribution fed from our own booking records.
14. 730-day audiences; one frequency cap per campaign; "push delivery to this ad".
15. Market backdrop: click rates up, conversion rates down; a dip from August.
