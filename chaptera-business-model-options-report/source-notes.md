# Source notes: Business Models That Fit Chaptera

Generated on 2026-08-05. The recommendation combines a repository audit with current official company, pricing, help-center, regulatory, and SEC sources.

## Chaptera product audit

Repository: `main` at commit `b08e130`.

Observed live capabilities:

- Three live offer modes: invite/application experiences, open paid events, and free WhatsApp/community events.
- Applications, approvals, advance/full payment, balance collection, waitlists, group entry, multi-city/date/pickup configuration, itinerary, accommodation, media, reviews, FAQs, capacity, and hosted PayU checkout.
- Admin operations for events, flow, people, team, growth, build, and settings.
- Creator attribution and commission dashboards; marketer and manager assignments and commission tracking; content tasks; WhatsApp/email/push recovery workflows.
- Chaptera acts as the customer-facing operator under its present product and terms.

Material gaps for marketplace or SaaS models:

- No complete self-serve host/supplier workspace, multi-tenant organization model, host KYC/tax onboarding, split payout system, or host inventory controls.
- No complete automated refund/cancellation/chargeback and seller-settlement ledger.
- No recurring membership billing/entitlements or durable member wallet/account surface.
- Atomic capacity reservation, multi-ticket/group purchase, dynamic pricing, referrals/credits, and broader loyalty mechanics are proposals or partial foundations rather than complete live systems.
- Current performance logic is not a full event-date P&L; event fixed costs, labor, refunds, chargebacks, comps, taxes, incomplete balances, and founder time need explicit treatment.

## Scoring method

Each model was scored from 1 to 5 on six factors and normalized to 100:

- Current product/team leverage: 25%
- Customer and brand fit: 20%
- Economics and cash quality: 20%
- Repeatability: 15%
- Speed to test: 10%
- Lower execution/regulatory risk: 10%

These are transparent strategic judgments, not forecasts. Rankings should be recomputed after Chaptera has event-date contribution, refund leakage, acquisition incrementality, repeat cohorts, venue economics, B2B sales data, and founder/team time.

## Evidence quality and interpretation

SEC filings and regulator materials are the strongest sources for reported scale, risk, and legal mechanics. Official help/pricing/terms pages are strong for current commercial mechanics. Company case studies and marketing claims are useful for identifying mechanisms but remain self-reported. All external fee percentages are market anchors, not direct pricing recommendations for Chaptera or India.

No live Chaptera financial or customer-level dataset was used. The core unknowns are fully loaded event contribution, repeat contribution, acquisition incrementality, venue willingness to pay, B2B pipeline quality, and whether non-founder captains can match Chaptera's quality and safety baseline.
